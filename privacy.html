Bill Mate

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 3, 2026

Overview

Bill Mate helps you organize bills, payment schedules, payment history, and receipt photos. The app is designed to keep this information on your device. It does not require an account and does not send your bill or payment information to a Bill Mate server.

Information stored by the app

Depending on how you use Bill Mate, it stores the following on your device:

  • Bill details such as names, categories, amounts, due dates, and recurring schedule settings.
  • Payment details such as amounts, dates, methods, notes, and paths to receipt photos you choose to attach.
  • App preferences such as currency, dark mode, notification settings, reminder time, and onboarding progress.

Bill data and preferences are stored using the device's local preferences storage. On Android and iOS, receipt photos attached to payments are copied to the app's private documents storage. The app does not sync this information between devices.

Permissions and device features

  • Notifications: If you allow notifications, Bill Mate schedules reminders on your device for unpaid payments due today, at the reminder time you select. Reminder schedules are managed locally by the operating system.
  • Camera: Camera access is requested when you choose to take a receipt photo.
  • Photos and media: The system photo picker lets you choose a receipt image. If you choose to save or download a payment photo to your gallery, the app may request the required operating-system access.

You can manage these permissions in your device settings. Turning off a permission may make its associated feature unavailable.

Sharing and third parties

Bill Mate does not include an account service, advertising SDK, analytics service, or app-operated cloud backup. The app does not transmit your bills, payments, notes, or receipt photos to us.

The operating system handles permission prompts, notifications, camera and photo selection, and any device backups you have enabled. If you export a photo to your gallery or include app data in a device backup, that copy is governed by your device settings and the applicable platform provider's terms and privacy policy.

Retention and deletion

Your data stays on your device until you delete it. You can delete bills and their associated payment records and receipt photos through the app's Settings. Deleting a bill does not necessarily remove a photo you separately saved to your device gallery or copies retained in a device backup. Uninstalling the app may remove its local data; backup and gallery copies may remain.

Security

App data is stored using the device's app-private storage mechanisms. Protect your device with a screen lock and keep its operating system up to date. No method of storage is completely secure, and Bill Mate cannot protect data after it has been exported or copied outside the app.

Children's privacy

Bill Mate is a general bill-tracking utility and is not designed to collect personal information from children. Because the app does not send entered bill information to us, we do not receive that information through the app.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the app's features or data practices change. The effective date above indicates when this version was last updated. Please review the policy again after a material app update.
